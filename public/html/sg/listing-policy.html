Listing Policy & Marketplace Rules

Effective Date: 1 April 2026  ·  Applies to: All listings, sellers, and buyers on lokalads Singapore

This Listing Policy sets out what may and may not be posted on lokalads, and the standards every seller and buyer must follow to keep the marketplace safe and trustworthy. It works alongside our Terms & Conditions and is enforced under the Consumer Protection (Fair Trading) Act, the Online Safety (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act 2022, the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), the Children and Young Persons Act, the Tripartite Guidelines on Fair Employment Practices, and investment-scheme rules enforced by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS).

1. General Listing Standards

  • Information must be accurate, current, and honestly describe the item, property, or service
  • You must own the item or hold the legal right to sell, rent, or advertise it
  • Use real, unedited photos of the actual item — not stock images, AI-generated images, or someone else's photos
  • Post one listing per item — no duplicate or multi-category spam posts
  • Price must be stated clearly in Singapore Dollars (SGD) and reflect what you genuinely intend to accept
  • List in the correct category and subcategory so buyers can find it honestly

2. Never Allowed

The following are prohibited on lokalads in every category, with no exceptions:

  • Weapons, ammunition, or explosive materials
  • Illegal drugs, controlled substances, or drug paraphernalia
  • Counterfeit, replica, or knock-off goods
  • Stolen goods or items you cannot prove ownership of
  • Protected wildlife, wildlife products, or endangered species
  • Adult content or sexual services
  • Hazardous, flammable, or radioactive materials
  • Anything that exploits or trafficks people, including child labour
  • Any content that endangers, sexualises, or exploits a child — zero tolerance, escalated immediately and reported under the Children and Young Persons Act, no exceptions
  • Listings, wording, or criteria that discriminate against a buyer, tenant, or applicant on grounds covered by the Tripartite Guidelines on Fair Employment Practices (e.g. race, religion, age, family status)
  • Sharing another person's private information, photos, or documents without their consent (doxxing)
  • Prescription-only medicines sold without a licence
  • Products recalled or banned by the Health Sciences Authority (HSA) or other Singapore regulators
  • Personal identity documents (NRIC, passports, employment/work passes)
  • Pirated software, media, game keys, or unauthorised digital downloads
  • AI-generated or deepfake images/video used to misrepresent a person, product, or condition
  • Unlicensed investment schemes, crypto trading "signals," or guaranteed-return offers not authorised by MAS

3. Allowed With Conditions

  • Pets — must comply with the Animals and Birds Act and AVS licensing rules; no protected or exotic species
  • Vehicles — valid LTA registration transfer, a valid COE, and inspection certificate must be available on request
  • Property — you must hold legal right to sell/rent; HDB and URA rules apply depending on property type; no discriminatory tenant or buyer criteria
  • Jobs — no fees may be charged to jobseekers; no pyramid or MLM schemes disguised as employment
  • Regulated services — medical, legal, and financial service listings must display a valid licence/registration number
  • Ticket resale — must comply with the Ticket Sale (Sports Events) Act where applicable
  • Alcohol, tobacco & vapes — vapes are banned outright in Singapore; alcohol/tobacco require the seller to hold a valid licence and the buyer to meet the legal age
  • Restricted or licensed goods — replica weapons and certain medical devices may only be listed where the seller holds the required licence and discloses it on request

4. Category-Specific Guidance

  • Property — Never send a deposit before viewing the property in person or via a live video walkthrough. No discriminatory tenant/buyer criteria.
  • Vehicles — Chassis/engine number must match the logbook; odometer readings must never be altered.
  • Jobs — Real employer, job role, and pay range must be stated; "pay to apply" is always a scam. No discriminatory hiring criteria (Tripartite Guidelines).
  • Services — No guaranteed-return investment, trading, or "get rich quick" advice.
  • Pets — Always see the animal in person before paying; be wary of "shipping" a pet you haven't met.
  • Business — Business-for-sale listings must share genuine financials on request; franchises must not promise guaranteed income.
  • Community — Lost & found reunification must never require payment; event/volunteering posts must not solicit upfront fees to unverified accounts.
  • Special Offers — Discounts must reflect a genuine prior price; "limited time" urgency must be truthful.
  • Education — Tutors and course providers must not claim guaranteed exam results or guaranteed visa/admission outcomes.
  • Health & Beauty — No unverified medical claims; licensed practitioners must show their MOH/HSA registration.
  • Food & Dining — Home chefs and caterers must disclose allergen information and hold an SFA licence where required.
  • Travel & Stays — Never pay the full amount via crypto or gift cards before travel; short-term rental hosts must comply with URA short-term stay rules.
  • Baby & Kids — No recalled or unsafe baby gear (car seats, cots, feeding products).
  • Sports & Outdoors — Safety-critical gear (climbing, water sports) must have any damage or wear disclosed.
  • Electronics & Tech — Devices must not be stolen or IMEI-blacklisted; disclose refurbished/used condition; no counterfeit chargers or batteries.
  • Home & Furniture — No recalled appliances or known fire-hazard items.
  • Fashion & Clothing — No counterfeit "designer" goods; material and authenticity must be accurately described.
  • Musical Instruments — Disclose any restoration work or non-original parts on vintage/rare instruments.
  • Books, Media & Collectibles — No pirated media, software, or game keys.
  • Tickets & Vouchers — Never pay before receiving a valid, transferable ticket or QR code.
  • Free & Giveaway — A genuinely free listing should never request a "delivery fee" or "reservation deposit" — that is a common scam pattern.

5. Buyer & Seller Safety Guidance

  • Meet in a safe, public place for high-value in-person exchanges
  • Inspect the item before paying — never pay for delivery in advance to a stranger
  • Never share OTPs, PayNow/PayLah PINs, or full card details with anyone, including "verified" buyers
  • Be cautious of AI-generated photos, cloned voices, or deepfake video calls used to build false trust
  • Verify identity through a live video call before paying for high-value items
  • Be sceptical of prices far below fair market value — it is the most common scam signal
  • Never scan an unfamiliar QR code to pay a "refundable deposit"
  • Keep conversations in the lokalads chat where possible — it helps us assist you if something goes wrong
  • Report suspicious accounts, listings, or profile photos immediately using the Report button, or via ScamShield if you believe you have been targeted by fraud
  • No genuine investment carries a "guaranteed return" — treat any crypto, forex, or trading scheme promising fixed profits as a scam
  • Never post another person's address, phone number, or photos without their consent, even in a dispute

6. Enforcement

Violations are handled in stages:

  • Under review — a reported listing is temporarily hidden from search while our team investigates
  • Listing blocked — removed from the platform; sellers may appeal through Support
  • Account suspended — repeated or severe violations result in account-level action
  • Zero-tolerance escalation — reports involving child safety bypass standard review and escalate immediately to the Ministry of Social and Family Development (MSF) and the Singapore Police Force
  • Reported to authorities — illegal activity is reported to the Singapore Police Force or relevant regulator

7. Reporting a Listing or User

Use the Report button on any listing or profile. You'll receive a ticket ID and our Trust & Safety team reviews reports promptly, prioritising scams, fraud, and illegal content.
